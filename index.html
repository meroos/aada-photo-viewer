About this demo

This page shows stone artefacts from the Archaeological Artefact Database of Finland (AADA) that have a published photograph. Finds come from a snapshot of the AADA layer on the University of Turku GeoNode (geodata.utu.fi, taken on 10 November 2026). Photographs are read on demand from the AADA photo deposit on Zenodo: the page requests only the bytes of the chosen photo from inside the published zip archives, so no copy of the photographs is hosted here.

Cite the data: Pesonen, P., Moilanen, U., Roose, M., Saipio, J., Tiilikkala, J., Sanwal, U., Immonen, V., Vesakoski, O. & Onkamo, P. 2024. Archaeological Artefact Database of Finland (AADA). Scientific Data 11, 815. doi:10.1038/s41597-024-03602-8

Photographs: AADA Photographs, Zenodo, doi:10.5281/zenodo.11256533. Licence as stated in the deposit: CC BY 4.0.

Limitations: photographs are full resolution (typically about 0.5 MB), so each takes a moment to load. Only stone artefacts are included in this demo. Raw-material names are given in Finnish as recorded in AADA.
